  • We handle only the data that signing in, using Siikli and being a customer require.
  • We don't sell data or use it for advertising.

2. What this policy covers

This policy explains how we handle personal data

  • in the Siikli app at app.siikli.fi
  • on the website siikli.fi
  • when you contact us or are our customer.

3. The app's users

A customer company's administrator adds the company's users to Siikli. For each user, Siikli stores:

  • a username and a role, such as office or warehouse
  • a hash of the password, if the user signs in with a password. We never store or see the password itself.
  • an email address and a Google account ID, if the user signs in with Google (section 4).

We use this data to sign users in and to check what each user may see and do. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in providing the service to the customer company and its users under our agreement.

For every request, the server logs the time, the IP address, the address requested and the response status. We use the logs to find faults and to monitor security, and they are deleted after 90 days. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a working and secure service.

4. Signing in with Google

When you sign in with Google, Google gives Siikli your email address, your Google account ID and your basic profile (your name and profile picture). Siikli does not ask for access to your Gmail, Drive, calendar, contacts or any other data in your Google account.

We use this data only to sign you in: we check that the email address belongs to a user your company has added to Siikli, and then sign you in. We store only the email address and the Google account ID with your user. They are deleted when your user is deleted.

We don't sell data received from Google, share it with anyone, or use it for advertising or to train AI models.

Siikli's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Google handles the sign-in as a controller in its own right, under the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Companies' own data in Siikli

A customer company stores its own business data in Siikli: its customers and their contact details, products, prices, orders, lots and invoices. This can include personal data, such as the names of contact persons or the details of sole traders.

The customer company is the controller of this data. We process it on the company's behalf and on its instructions, as agreed in the terms of service. To exercise your rights over this data, contact the company whose customer you are. We help them when needed.

6. Contacting us and being a customer

When you contact us by phone, text or email, we handle your name, your company, your contact details and what you write to us. We use them to reply, to show you Siikli, and to look after our customer relationship. Our sales are handled by Olli Tuovinen, who handles contacts on our behalf.

We also contact companies that Siikli might suit. We then use the company's public contact details, for example from its website. If you don't want us to contact you, tell us and we won't contact you again.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in replying to you and in offering Siikli to businesses. While you are a customer, it is our agreement. We keep invoicing and accounting records because the Finnish Accounting Act requires it.

7. The website and cookies

siikli.fi sets no cookies and uses no analytics, ad tracking or scripts from other services. Even its fonts are served from siikli.fi itself. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs visitors' IP addresses for security under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

The Siikli app uses two strictly necessary cookies: a sign-in cookie that keeps you signed in, and a security cookie that blocks requests sent from other sites. Siikli has no tracking or advertising cookies.

8. Who handles the data

We don't sell data or share it for marketing. We use these service providers:

  • DigitalOcean: Siikli's server, in Amsterdam, the Netherlands.
  • Amazon Web Services: backups, in Stockholm, Sweden. They are encrypted on the server first, and only we hold the key, so AWS cannot read them.
  • Google: our email (Gmail), and sign-in with Google.
  • GitHub: the website siikli.fi.
  • Visma: only if the customer uses the Fivaldi add-on. Invoices then go to the customer's own Fivaldi, under the customer's agreement with Visma.

The app's data and backups are stored in the EU. DigitalOcean, Amazon, Google and GitHub are US companies, however. Where data is transferred outside the EU, the transfer relies on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

We disclose data to authorities only when the law requires it.

9. How long data is kept

  • User data: as long as the user exists in Siikli. It is deleted when an administrator deletes the user or when the customer leaves.
  • Logs: 90 days.
  • Backups: hourly backups for 7 days, daily for 90 days and monthly for 2 years. No one can delete a backup early, not even us, so deleted data disappears from the backups within two years at most. Backups are used only to restore data.
  • When a customer leaves, we hand over its data and delete it from the server within 30 days.
  • Contacts: as long as the matter needs. We delete them on request.
  • Invoicing and accounting records: as long as the Finnish Accounting Act requires.

10. Security

  • All connections to Siikli are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • The app opens only from Finland, and the server can be administered only from the administrator's own address.
  • Only a hash of each password is stored, and failed sign-in attempts are limited.
  • On every request, the server checks that the user's role allows it.
  • Data is backed up, encrypted, every hour.
  • Security updates are installed automatically, and Siikli is checked for security holes every day.

If a security breach affects your personal data, we will tell you and the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman as the law requires.

11. Your rights

You have the right to

  • know what data we hold about you, and get a copy of it
  • have incorrect data corrected
  • have your data deleted, or its processing restricted
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest, including direct marketing
  • get the data you gave us in a machine-readable format.

Send your request by email to juha.wilppu@gmail.com. We reply within a month. If the request is about a customer company's data in Siikli (section 5), we pass it on to that company.

If you think we handle your data unlawfully, you can complain to the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman: tietosuoja.fi.

12. Changes

We update this policy when the way we handle data changes. The date of the last update is at the top. We tell our customers about significant changes in advance.
