A simple order, packing-list and invoicing system for wholesale food deliveries. Why Siikli

Siikli is simple on purpose. It does orders, packing lists, invoices and sales reports, and not much more.

From order to invoice

The app is in Finnish.

Traceability

Record your field plots and lots, and every delivered product's lot and plot are printed on the waybill. Siikli suggests the lot on each order row, and each lot shows which customers it went to.

Sales reports

Every sale of a period, row by row, downloaded as an Excel file.

Two roles

The office sees everything. The warehouse sees only the packing lists, without prices.

Carefully maintained

Your data is safe

Your data is backed up every hour, encrypted, and no one can delete the backups early, not even me. Siikli is checked for security holes every day, and I usually fix any findings the same day.

Straight from the maker

I built Siikli in 2017 and have maintained it ever since. Your questions are answered by the person who built it.
