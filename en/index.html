Siikli

A simple order, packing-list and invoicing system for wholesale food deliveries.

Take orders for each delivery date, print clear lists for the packing room, and put a customer's deliveries on one invoice. A Finnish farm has used Siikli every day for nine years, and €17 million has been invoiced through it.

Why Siikli

Siikli is simple on purpose. It does orders, packing lists, invoices and sales reports, and not much more.

I once recommended that the farm move to a full ERP. They wanted to keep Siikli, and told me why:

From order to invoice

The app is in Finnish. The data on this page is demo data.

Sales reports

Every sale of a period, row by row, downloaded as an Excel file.

Two roles

The office sees everything. The warehouse sees only the packing lists, without prices.

Carefully maintained

Backups every hour

Your data is backed up every hour, encrypted. No one can delete the backups early, not even me.

Checked against real invoices

When Siikli was upgraded in 2026, every invoice, packing list and sales report since 2017 was run on both the old and the new version and compared value by value. The new version went live only when they matched.

A secure server

HTTPS, a firewall, automatic security updates and monitoring. The server checks on every request what the user may see.

Straight from the maker

I built Siikli in 2017 and have maintained it ever since. Your questions are answered by the person who built it.

Get started

Siikli is a good fit if you

  • deliver orders to shops, restaurants or wholesalers on set days
  • need clear lists for the packing room of what to pack and for whom
  • invoice deliveries afterwards, for example once a month
  • want these done well, without a heavy ERP
