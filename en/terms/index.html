These terms apply to the Siikli service at app.siikli.fi from the moment the customer takes it into use. Where we have agreed otherwise with a customer in writing, including by email, that agreement takes precedence.

2. The service

Siikli is an order, packing-list and invoicing system used in a web browser. Its features are described at siikli.fi. The price includes the server, backups, updates and support.

Siikli is in Finnish and opens only from Finland. It cannot be reached from abroad.

We keep developing Siikli. We tell customers about significant changes in advance.

3. Users

The customer decides who uses Siikli. Its administrator adds and removes users. The customer can have as many users as it needs.

The customer is responsible for what its users do in Siikli, and for keeping safe the passwords and Google accounts used to sign in. If an account falls into the wrong hands, tell us at once.

Siikli must not be used unlawfully, and no one may try to get around its security.

4. Trial, price and invoicing

A new customer uses Siikli free of charge for the first three months. If the customer doesn't want to continue, it is enough to tell us before the trial ends. Nothing is charged for the trial.

After the trial, the price is the monthly price shown at siikli.fi, unless agreed otherwise. VAT is added to the prices. We invoice monthly, with 14 days net payment terms. Late payments bear interest under the Finnish Interest Act.

The Fivaldi add-on has its own monthly price. It needs the customer's own Fivaldi subscription, which the customer pays to Visma and which is under Visma's terms.

We announce price changes at least 30 days in advance. If the customer doesn't accept a change, it can cancel the agreement to end on the day the change would take effect.

The data stored in Siikli belongs to the customer. We use it only to provide the service, give support and fix faults.

The customer can have all of its data as Excel files at any time on request, including when the agreement ends.

The customer is responsible for having the right to store in Siikli the data it stores there. The customer is also responsible for checking invoices before sending them.

6. Processing of personal data

When personal data is stored in Siikli, the customer is its controller and we are the processor. This section is the agreement on processing personal data under Article 28 of the EU General Data Protection Regulation.

  • What and why: data about the customer's customers, their contact persons and the customer's users, usually names, addresses, contact details, and order and invoicing data. We process it only to provide Siikli, for as long as the agreement lasts.
  • Instructions: we process the data only on the customer's instructions. These terms and the customer's actions in Siikli are those instructions. If the law requires other processing of us, we tell the customer, unless the law forbids it.
  • Confidentiality: only those whose work requires it handle the data, and they are bound by confidentiality.
  • Security: we protect the data as described in the privacy policy.
  • Subprocessors: DigitalOcean (the server, Amsterdam), Amazon Web Services (encrypted backups, Stockholm) and Google (email, if the customer sends data by email). The customer approves them. We tell the customer about new subprocessors in advance, and the customer can object or cancel the agreement.
  • Assistance: we help the customer reply to data subjects' requests and meet its other obligations, such as security, breach notification and impact assessments.
  • Breaches: we tell the customer about a personal data breach without undue delay.
  • When the agreement ends: we hand the data over to the customer and delete it from the server within 30 days. It disappears from the backups when their retention ends, within two years at most.
  • Audits: we give the customer the information it needs to show that the obligations are met, and allow reasonable audits. Audits are agreed in advance, at the customer's cost.

Security updates are installed at night. We announce longer maintenance breaks in advance.

Support is included in the price, by phone, text and email. Faults that stop orders, packing lists or invoices from being made are fixed first.

The agreement is in force until further notice. Either party can cancel it with one month's notice. Notice can be given by email.

When the agreement ends, the customer gets its data as Excel files, and we delete it as described in section 6.
