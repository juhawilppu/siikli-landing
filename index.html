Siikli

An order, invoicing and packing-list system I built for a Finnish farm and wholesaler, and still run and maintain.

Their office takes orders and invoices customers with it, and their packing room packs each day's deliveries from its lists. In nine years of daily use, €17 million has been invoiced through it.

Why Siikli

Siikli is a simple system, almost embarrassingly so. It does orders, packing lists, invoices and sales reports, and not much more.
